## 2026-07 — Measurement conventions corrected (read before older sections)

Three conventions changed on `feature/measurement-truth`; **all tables above/below this
section that predate it use the old conventions and are not comparable**:

1. **SNR is now 3 kHz-referenced against clean signal power** (ITU HF convention, matching
   MIL-STD/FreeDV/VARA-style figures). Numerically this adds 10·log10(24000/3000) ≈ 9.0 dB
   of noise at the same nominal dB vs the old full-Nyquist, post-fade-referenced numbers —
   old tables read ≈ 9 dB flattering.
2. **Watterson fading is now ensemble-true Rayleigh**: per-frame received power varies and
   deep flat fades cost SNR (previously output power was renormalized per frame, making
   flat-fade outage impossible). Fading FERs are now higher and honest.
3. **Doppler presets follow ITU-R F.1487's two-sigma spread convention** (PSD sigma =
   spread/2); the named presets now fade at half the previous rate, matching the standard
   channels they are named after.

FER thresholds in re-baselined tables require the **Wilson 95% upper bound** to clear the
target (an "FER ≤ 1%" claim needs 0 errors in ≥ 368 trials, not 0-in-50).

On the Moderate channel, three modes — BPSK 1/4, BPSK 1/2, and QPSK 1/2 — clear the
FER≤10% Wilson-CI bar at all four of the highest tested SNR points (21, 24, 27, 30 dB), and
by 30 dB comfortably so (fer_hi ≈ 0.058–0.085, well under the 0.10 bar), not marginally; none
of the nine modes clears the stricter FER≤1% bar at any tested SNR on Moderate (best case
fer_hi ≈ 0.032 at 27 dB for BPSK 1/4). Watterson-Poor, by contrast, never clears the
FER≤10%/1% Wilson-CI bar at any tested SNR for any mode — Poor shows a genuine irreducible
outage floor from real ensemble-Rayleigh flat fading: e.g. BPSK 1/4 FER holds at 24.5–28.5%
and BPSK 1/2 at 41–45% across 24–30 dB (see `results/rebaseline-2026-07/poor.csv`),
confirming deep fades now cost real receive power instead of being renormalized away.
